# Listening sentence attribution

English sentences and Mandarin translations in `listening-bank.js` come from
the [Tatoeba Project](https://tatoeba.org/), downloaded via the
[ManyThings Mandarin-English sentence pairs](https://www.manythings.org/anki/).
Each bank item retains the original sentence identifiers and contributor
attribution in its `credit` field. The corpus text is licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/deed.en).
The selection was filtered for classroom use and paired with newly generated
British-English Sonia voice audio. Audio is not the Tatoeba recording.
